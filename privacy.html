Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Summary

Iris runs locally on the user's own computer. There is no Iris server, no account with us, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share your data.

Google user data

  • Access: With your consent, Iris accesses your Gmail messages, labels, drafts and settings and your Google Calendar events through Google's APIs.
  • Use: The data is used only to show, search, organize, compose and send your email and to show and manage your calendar events on your own device.
  • Storage: A local cache is stored only on your own computer. OAuth tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on that computer.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not transferred to anyone, except when you explicitly use an AI feature (for example "summarize this thread"): then only the content needed for that request is sent to Anthropic's Claude API under your own API key. Iris does not use Google user data to train AI models.
  • Limited Use: Iris's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Disconnect the account in Iris (Settings → Accounts) to delete its local cache and tokens, and revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google consent screen for Iris.
