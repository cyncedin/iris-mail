Iris

Iris is a personal, keyboard-first email and calendar client for Gmail and Google Calendar. It runs entirely on the user's own Mac as a local app and is not offered as a hosted service.

What it does

  • Reads, searches and organizes Gmail (archive, labels, reminders, drafts, sending).
  • Shows and edits Google Calendar events, including replying to invitations.
  • Lets the user's own AI assistants work with their mail through a local interface.

Privacy policy

Personal project. Not affiliated with Google or Superhuman.
